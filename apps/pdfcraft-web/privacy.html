Command PDF privacy

Command PDF's browser editor processes the PDFs you open in your browser. The editor does not upload your opened PDF contents to a Command PDF document server.

Saving downloads a file to your device. Keep the downloaded file in the company-approved location for that document. The browser version does not save back to email attachments, OneDrive or SharePoint.

Your browser can retain application preferences. DigitalOcean serves the application files and may receive ordinary web-request information. In Outlook and Teams, Microsoft's host and SDK receive application lifecycle messages; the Command PDF integration does not send them your PDF contents.

During the pilot, use synthetic test PDFs until saving and printing are accepted in your client. Follow MCM's existing document handling requirements.
